The Mother-Son Laser Tag Party is an exciting annual event that gives moms, sons, and loved ones the opportunity to enjoy a memorable evening together. The event includes three hours of laser tag, music, giveaways, food, and drinks. All mother figures welcome — open to residents and non-residents.
