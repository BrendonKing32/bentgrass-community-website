question: "What is Bent Grass / Falcon Meadows at Bent Grass?"
category: "Getting Started"
order: 1
---

Bent Grass Meadows is a Challenger Homes community in the Falcon/Peyton area of unincorporated El Paso County, Colorado. Built beginning in 2017, the community is essentially complete as of 2026. It's located within School District 49, near Woodmen Hills parks, the Powers corridor, and military bases.
