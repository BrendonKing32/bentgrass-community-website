---
question: "Do I need approval before making exterior changes to my home?"
category: "Covenants & Property Rules"
order: 3
---

Yes. The CC&Rs require Architectural Committee approval for fences, sheds, decks, paint colors, landscaping, additions, and similar work. Residents submit through the online Architectural Request Form or a printable form at [bentgrassmd.colorado.gov](https://bentgrassmd.colorado.gov).
