---
question: "Should I prepare an emergency kit?"
category: "Safety & Emergencies"
order: 3
---

Yes. Kits should include water/food (one gallon per person daily for three days), power sources (flashlights, batteries, radio), warmth items, first-aid supplies, prescriptions, important documents, a wildfire evacuation plan, and a family communication agreement. Consult El Paso County's Office of Emergency Management for detailed guidance.
