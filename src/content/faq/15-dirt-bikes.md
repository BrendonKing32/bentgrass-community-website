---
question: "Can my children ride dirt bikes, motorcycles, or e-bikes around the neighborhood?"
category: "Property Use"
order: 4
---

Dirt bikes/ATVs can't be ridden on public streets (fines up to $500). Gas/electric motorcycles require licensed, endorsed, insured operators. E-bikes follow a three-class system; Class 3 requires riders to be 16+ and helmeted if under 18. Adults who knowingly allow illegal vehicle operation face Contributing to the Delinquency charges. Consult the El Paso County Sheriff's Office for specifics.
