question: "Which district covers my property, and what do the districts do?"
category: "Districts & Governance"
order: 1
---

Two independent metropolitan districts operate here: Bent Grass Metropolitan District (BGMD) handles covenants, architectural review, parks, and drainage. Woodmen Hills Metropolitan District (WHMD) provides water/sewer service and larger recreation facilities. Confirm your own property by looking up your address on the [El Paso County Assessor's property search](https://property.spatialest.com/co/elpaso/#/).
