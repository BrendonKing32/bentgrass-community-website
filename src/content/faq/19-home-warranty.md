Submit requests through Challenger Homes' warranty team via their online form, or contact their main office at 8605 Explorer Drive, Suite 250, Colorado Springs, (719) 598-5192 (Monday–Friday, 8 a.m.–5 p.m.). Challenger provides 2-10 Home Buyers Warranty coverage. Report issues promptly — coverage windows close at one and two years respectively.
