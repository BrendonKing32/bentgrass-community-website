---
question: "What are the Covenants, Conditions, and Restrictions (CC&Rs)?"
category: "Covenants & Property Rules"
order: 1
---

The CC&Rs are your neighborhood's recorded rules — covering home appearance, landscaping, fencing, and what you can build or change on your property. They're administered by BGMD and recorded with El Paso County. Separate declarations exist for different filings, available through the Bent Grass District Resources page.
