---
question: "Can I fly a drone over the neighborhood?"
category: "Property Use"
order: 2
---

Generally yes under FAA rules (below 400 feet, within visual line of sight, registered if over 0.55 lbs). Colorado law criminalizes drone photography of people in private places without consent. Don't fly over county parks, Colorado Springs parks (without permission), state/national parks, or military airspace near Schriever SFB, Peterson SFB, or the Air Force Academy. Use the FAA B4UFLY app before flying.
