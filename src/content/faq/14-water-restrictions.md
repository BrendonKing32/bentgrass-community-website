---
question: "Do I have water restrictions during summer?"
category: "Property Use"
order: 3
---

Yes. WHMD's Stage 1 restrictions apply year-round: even-numbered addresses water Sunday/Wednesday/Friday; odd-numbered addresses water Saturday/Tuesday/Thursday. Watering occurs only before 9 a.m. or after 6 p.m. Stage 2 reduces to two days weekly; Stage 3 prohibits outside watering. Penalties range from warning letters to $500 fines and service termination.
