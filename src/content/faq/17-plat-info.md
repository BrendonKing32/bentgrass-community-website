---
question: "What is a neighborhood plat?"
category: "Property, Plots & Lot Info"
order: 2
---

A plat is the official, recorded map of a subdivision. It defines lot dimensions, boundaries, streets, easements, and shared tracts, and shows property lines critical before building. Five filings exist: Filing No. 1 (Reception No. 221714886), Filing No. 2 (223715087), Filing No. 2A (224715386), Filing No. 3 (224715349), and Filing No. 4 (224715350).
