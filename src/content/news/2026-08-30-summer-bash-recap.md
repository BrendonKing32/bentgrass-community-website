date: 2026-08-30

The Summer Bash successfully brought neighbors together with games, face painting, and a water war. Together we raised **$270 for wildfire relief** through United Way of Southern Colorado.

Thank you to the local vendors and sponsors who made it happen: HipPOPs, Los Tres Perros, Firehouse Subs, Walmart, Slim Chickens, and Swirly Cow Frozen Yogurt. Falcon Fire Station 3 personnel also attended and gave tours of their emergency vehicles.

Thanks to everyone who came out — see you at the next one!
